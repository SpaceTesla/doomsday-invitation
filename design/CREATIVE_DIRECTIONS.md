# Creative directions for the Latverian summons

Phase 1.5. Three visual directions built on the Phase 1 research, each with a concept board in `design/explorations/`. This document defines the directions; `COMPARISON.md` scores them and gives the recommendation. Nothing here is the final design language, and no application, architecture or backend decision is made.

## What the research gives us to work with

**The strongest ideas**

- **The object is a state document.** Doom acts through statecraft, and real chancery practice supplies a form the fiction lacks: a titled first line, a sanction clause, a monogram, a pendant seal.
- **Restraint is the look.** The two film frames are about half near-black. Green is light and air, not paint. Gold is the colour of light. Steel is the neutral. One saturated accent sits in a very small area.
- **Latveria's trait is concealment.** A storybook medieval shell with the machinery hidden until it moves.
- **Black vellum is a real court palette.** The black books of hours (Bruges, c. 1455–1480) put silver text and gold initials on black, one of them with gold initials on green grounds.
- **Canon heraldry is plural**, so an original crest is legitimate.
- **Motion matters at five beats:** the opening, the gate, the guest's name, entering the council, the approval.

**The contradictions each direction has to take a side on**

| Tension | The options |
|---|---|
| What "gothic" means | Late Gothic as in the Carpathian castles; the film's Art Nouveau and Art Deco at Gothic scale; Gothic reduced to "the idea of a castle" |
| Which Doom | The comics' monarch and his court; the film's light and costume |
| The green | The film's measured olive and sage; a cleaner, bluer emerald |
| How technology shows | Not at all; only as light; a brief machine voice |
| How much blackletter | None; one glyph; a headline |
| The document | A letter sealed shut for one person (a letter close); an open proclamation with a hanging seal (letters patent) |
| The first five seconds | An approach to the castle; the hall as a still; one object emerging from black |

**The opportunities**

- No award-level seal-break or invitation precedent was found, so the gate interaction is open ground.
- The supplied library has no exterior, heraldry, document or typography. Those parts will be original whichever direction is chosen.
- The black-and-gold, Cinzel-and-Cormorant look is a sold template. All three directions avoid it on purpose.

## What all three directions hold constant

These come straight from the research and are not up for comparison.

- A tinted near-black base with off-white text at 13:1 or better. No pure black or pure white in text pairs.
- The face stays hidden. No visible eyes, no glowing eyes.
- One original insignia per direction, drawn at two levels of detail. No studio logo, no film still, and none of the seven reference images appears in any board.
- The two clasp emblems from the film costume (the star and the square-cross) are not redrawn, because press reports read them as other characters' marks.
- State is never signalled by red against green alone.
- The summons follows the charter formula, in Doom's third-person voice.

**Sample copy used on every board**, so the boards differ in form and not in words. The guest name and the date are placeholders.

> **Victor von Doom**, by his own hand and will Sovereign of Latveria, Lord of Doomstadt and of all lands within the Carpathian ring,
> to **Tara Menon**, greeting.
>
> Be it known that Doom has weighed the defenders of this world and found them wanting; that he now gathers a company of his own choosing; and that your name stands upon the roll.
>
> You are therefore summoned to take your seat at the Council of Doom, where the matters set before it shall be weighed and approved.
>
> Let none who is called fail to answer. Doom keeps his word, and the absent forfeit their voice.
>
> In witness whereof we have caused our seal to be set to these letters. Given at Castle Doom, in Doomstadt, the fifteenth day of November, MMXXVI.

The closing action is named **Take your seat** on every board.

## Direction I — The Winter Chancery

*Starting point: the Kingdom of Latveria, historically grounded Central European Gothic.*

Board: `explorations/direction-1-winter-chancery.html`

### Creative concept and visual identity

A letter has come from a real place. Latveria is treated as a small Carpathian monarchy with a six-hundred-year-old chancery, and the guest receives a **letter close**: a sheet of black vellum written in silver, sealed shut in green wax, addressed to one person. Nothing modern is visible anywhere. The menace is the quiet of a snowbound town under a castle whose one lit window never goes out.

The identity is the page, taken from the black books of hours: silver script, one gold initial on a green ground, fine gold ruling, a pendant seal.

### How it relates to Doom and Latveria

This is the comics' Latveria of 1964 and 1969: a fairy-tale village beneath a 16th-century castle, ringed by the Carpathians. It gives Doom the things canon never fixed for him, a chancery hand, a seal and a coat of arms, built from the motifs canon does supply (the eagle and the sceptre). It is the direction least dependent on the unreleased film, and the only one that shows Latveria as a country.

| Document ground | Vellum black | `#100F0E` | — |

The base is blue-black, which is the black hours' own colour and the colour of Bohemian slate roofs. It is cooler than the film frames. Green appears only as wax and as the ground of the initial. Red is the state's colour, after the one flag the comics source documents (red with a black eagle), and it is used as a rubric.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The one versal initial; seal monogram | **Grenze Gotisch** | Blackletter, one glyph at a time. Never a word, never capitals in a row |
| Engrossed first line, short display lines | **IM Fell English**, **IM Fell English SC** | Digitised 17th-century types with ink spread; gives the page a hand |
| Decree body | **EB Garamond** | 400 and 500; italic for the salutation |
| Labels, buttons, council interface | **Hanken Grotesk** | Quiet; small sizes only |

All four are on Google Fonts under the Open Font License.

### Architectural influences

Corvin Castle for the bridge approach and needle roofs. Hochosterwitz in winter for the isolated crag and the gate-after-gate climb. Karlštejn for stepped massing under one great tower with slate roofs. Biertan for the town: steep roofs and a fortified church. Late Gothic with Gothic Revival exaggeration, which is what the region's famous castles actually are. One pointed arch is allowed, as the frame of the page.

### Materials and textures

Black-dyed vellum with a faint uneven sheen. Silver and shell-gold ink. Green wax with a soft, irregular edge, on red-and-green silk cords. Slate, snow, and an engraver's hatched line for the arms. The hero material is **the wax seal**.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Cold moonlight from above and behind the castle. One warm window. Snow haze that eats the valley. Darkness is the default. Inside the letter there is no lighting effect at all; the page is flat and the metals do the work.

### Graphic motifs and insignia

- **Arms of Latveria:** a green shield, a silver eagle displayed, holding a gold sceptre; drawn in a single hatched line in the manner of Siebmacher's armorial, with a crested helm that repeats the eagle (the German and Nordic habit).
- **The seal:** round, the eagle in low relief, the legend running the rim: `SIGILLVM VICTORIS DOMINI LATVERIAE`. Reduced form: the eagle alone in a ring.
- The versal: gold on a green ground, four to six lines tall.
- Fine gold ruling under the text, as in the black hours.
- A calendar lunette (after the *Très Riches Heures*) to carry the date.
- Gates in sequence as the progress device, after Hochosterwitz's fourteen.

### Background treatment

A night gradient from blue-black to slate, layered ridges fading into haze, fine grain to stop banding, sparse snow. Behind the document: flat vellum black with the ruling, nothing else.

### Animation and sound direction

- Opening: the valley in darkness, the window lights, snow drifts.
- The gate: press and hold the seal; it cracks and the halves part.
- The page: lines ink in from left to right, quickly, then hold still. The guest's name is written last, after a held beat.
- Entering the council: a fade through black, as a gate.
- Approval: the seal is pressed.
- Sound: wind in a valley, one distant bell, the wax crack, the dry sound of vellum. No music.
- Structure: tap-advanced, one gate at a time.

### Invitation and decree composition

A portrait page in the black hours' architecture: a ruled text block, a wide margin, the versal "V" in gold on green, the first line engrossed in IM Fell capitals, the body in silver EB Garamond, the guest's name in gold, the sanction clause in rubric red, and the green seal hanging on its cords from a folded lower edge. On desktop the same letter is a landscape sheet with the arms in the margin.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**
- The most original and the most Latverian. Nothing in it can be overturned by the film's release.
- The document is the hero, which is where the research puts the centre of gravity.
- Built almost entirely from type and line, so it is light and sharp on any phone.
- Fills every gap in the reference library: exterior, heraldry, document, typography.

**Weaknesses**
- The least recognisably Doom at a glance: no figure, no green light, no steel.
- A castle in the snow can read as generic vampire country.
- Blackletter carries a real risk for a fictional authoritarian state, even at one glyph.
- The cool blue-black departs from the film's olive grade.
- Long passages of light text on black need care.

## Direction II — The Audience

*Starting point: the Imperial Court, luxurious royal and ceremonial aesthetics.*

Board: `explorations/direction-2-the-audience.html`

### Creative concept and visual identity

You are received at court. The first image is the hall itself: seven lit steps, a throne that rises out of frame, tall glass, two shafts of light crossing in the haze, and a small, still, hooded figure who does not get up. The summons is presented to you in that room as **letters patent**: an open sheet of warm parchment under a pool of gold light, with a gilt seal hanging from it.

Luxury here means scale, stillness and one precious object. The identity is the light.

### How it relates to Doom and Latveria

This is the film's Doom: the bowed sovereign in a cathedral-scale room, olive haze and amber light. It takes its architecture from the supplied frames (Art Nouveau glass and Art Deco geometry at Gothic scale) and its staging from real throne rooms (counted steps, one axis, the seat framed from behind). The ruler's cypher as the dominant mark on the sheet follows the Ottoman tughra; the gilt seal follows the Golden Bull of 1356, which is named for its seal.

| Shadow | Olive shadow | `#1B2417` | — |
| Cloth, deep fields | Velvet green | `#1C3324` | — |
| Haze, glass tint | Haze green | `#48603F` | 2.77 on base (atmosphere only) |
| Cool light | Sage light | `#AEB78D` | 9.14 on base |

| Warm light, the seal | Amber gold | `#C9A24A` | 8.03 on base |
| Ornament | Bronze | `#8F6B34` | 3.96 on base (large or ornament only) |

| The one small accent | Glyph green | `#5ED12F` | accent only, tiny area |

The greens are the film's measured olive and sage, not emerald. Gold never carries text on parchment (it fails at 2.01); on the sheet it is only the seal and the cypher's highlight.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Engrossed first line, titles | **Castoro Titling** | Inscriptional capitals, letterspaced. Chosen over Cinzel to stay off the template |
| Decree body, the guest's name | **Cormorant Garamond** | 500 for body on parchment; italic for the name at display size |
| Labels, buttons, council interface | **Instrument Sans** | Small sizes only |

All on Google Fonts under the Open Font License. No blackletter in this direction.

### Architectural influences

The supplied throne-room frame for glass, piers and light. Vladislav Hall for the single vanishing point. Aachen and the Winter Palace for counted steps. Neuschwanstein for the throne in the place of an altar. Karlštejn's Chapel of the Holy Cross for gold on shadow. Curved Nouveau leading in the windows; stepped Deco geometry in the throne.

### Materials and textures

Streaked, weathered glass. Polished dark stone that reflects the steps. Heavy green wool and velvet. Parchment with a soft tooth. The hero material is **the gilt seal**: irregular, dented, hand-cut, never mirror-bright.

### Lighting and atmosphere

Backlit. The windows are the key light, and throne and figure are silhouettes. A cool sage shaft from the upper left crosses a warm gold shaft from the upper right. Step edges catch gold. Two bowl lamps hold the corners. Warm means "toward the guest": the pool of light on the patent is the warmest thing on any screen.

### Graphic motifs and insignia

- **The cypher:** V and D interlaced under an open crown, large enough to dominate the sheet.
- **The gilt seal:** the sovereign enthroned and frontal, hooded, sceptre in hand, the legend around the rim: `VICTOR DEI NVLLIVS GRATIA DOMINVS LATVERIAE` ("by no god's grace"). Reduced form: the cypher in a ring.
- Seven horizontal lines, the counted steps, as the divider.
- A window-leading curve as a single corner device.
- The filigree panel above the throne as the one piece of dense ornament.

### Background treatment

The hall as a painted still, darkest at the edges and floor, with the light shafts as soft gradients over fine grain. Behind the document the hall is dimmed and blurred and a warm vignette falls on the sheet.

### Animation and sound direction

- Opening: the hall resolves out of black, shafts first.
- The gate: "Approach the throne." A slow push toward the steps; nothing else moves.
- The sheet: it is laid into the light. The engrossed line settles its letterspacing. The guest's name is written in oxblood, then takes one sheen.
- Entering the council: tall doors part.
- Approval: the seal swings once and settles.
- Sound: stone-hall room tone, a low drone, a single choir swell on the name, a heavy door.
- Structure: three scenes, tap-advanced.

### Invitation and decree composition

An upright parchment sheet in a pool of warm light, the hall dark behind it. The crowned cypher sits at the head. The first line is engrossed in Castoro capitals, the body is ink in Cormorant Garamond, the guest's name is large italic oxblood, and the gilt seal hangs on green cords from the folded foot. On desktop the sheet is landscape, with the cypher filling the left third like a tughra.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**
- The most cinematic, and the closest to the supplied references and to what guests will have seen in the trailer.
- The parchment sheet gives the best reading contrast of the three (12.69:1).
- The court gives the council a natural setting: you enter a room.
- Warm and cool light carry meaning without extra ornament.

**Weaknesses**
- It needs a painted hall to reach its ceiling, which is the heaviest artwork of the three.
- It sits closest to studio-owned design, and its details can be overturned on 18 December 2026.
- A wide, symmetrical hall is the hardest composition to keep on a phone.
- Gold and serif capitals can slide toward the luxury template.
- The throne room is the image everyone expects.

## Direction III — The Iron Protocol

*Starting point: the Technocratic Monarch, Gothic architecture combined with advanced technology.*

Board: `explorations/direction-3-iron-protocol.html`

### Creative concept and visual identity

The state speaks. Doom is treated as scientist and sovereign at once, and Latveria as an apparatus that has already identified you. The world is stone reduced to "the idea of a castle", and inside it is something precise: a hairline of emerald light where the machine is. The summons arrives as **an engraved steel plate**, the mask's own material, with bright letters reserved out of a blackened field and one line of machine voice at its head.

Technology shows only as light and as exactness. There are no panels, brackets, glitches or readouts. The identity is the plate and the ring.

### How it relates to Doom and Latveria

Kirby's costume is a regal tunic over armor, and this direction takes the armor's side: riveted plate, grey steel, brass rivets. Latveria's hidden sensors and robot guards ("nothing modern is visible until it moves") become a single line of light in a Gothic niche. The ring glyph comes from the film's floor inlay, and behaves like a seal: circular, with a legend on the rim. Wikipedia's coordinates for Latveria (46°N, 20.6°E) give the machine voice something true to say.

### Architectural influences

Pernštejn's tall, almost windowless wall seen from below. A single lancet niche. Aachen's plain throne of slabs held by bronze clamps. The production-design minimalists: tonal gradients painted up a column, forms reduced until only proportion is left. The throne's stepped, machined geometry from the film frames.

### Materials and textures

Blued and blackened steel plate, darker in the recesses and bare on the ridges. Brass rivets and one gilt edge strip. Niello turned inside out: bright letters in a black field. Dark stone with a tonal gradient. The hero material is **the plate**.

### Lighting and atmosphere

One cold key from high above, falling off fast. Emerald only where it has a visible source: the ring, a slit, a seam. No haze. The dark is clean, not smoky.

### Graphic motifs and insignia

- **The ring:** concentric circles, a square set in a rotated square at the centre (the costume's tile motif), radial ticks, and a legend on the rim in capitals: `LATVERIA · 46°N 20.6°E · DOOM`. Reduced form: the square-in-square inside one ring.
- The rivet line as the rule and divider.
- The plate seam: a hairline that can open.
- The hooded silhouette with a void for a face.
- A vertical slit of light.

### Background treatment

Near-flat Void with a slow vertical tonal gradient, as stone. No texture beyond fine grain. The only glow on any screen has a drawn source.

### Animation and sound direction

- Opening: black, then the ring is drawn as a line of light, once.
- The gate: "Hold to be recognised." The ring closes; the seam opens.
- The plate: letters resolve from blur to sharp, line by line. The guest's name lands, then one sheen crosses it.
- Entering the council: the plate seam parts like a door.
- Approval: a segment of the ring lights and stays lit.
- Sound: a low drone, an iron latch, a single deep mechanical seat. No beeps, no whooshes.
- Structure: tap and hold. The council scrolls natively.

### Invitation and decree composition

A portrait steel plate with a rivet line along each edge and a brass strip at the head. One line of machine voice, then the title in Bodoni, the body in Crimson Pro, the guest's name in large silver-white capitals with a hairline of emerald beneath it, and the ring at the foot. On desktop the plate is landscape: the ring fills the left third and the decree the right.

### Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**
- The most distinct from fantasy, and the only direction that includes Doom the scientist.
- Portrait-native: the standing figure and the plate both fit a phone without a second composition.
- The lightest to build: lines, plates and one light. Nothing needs a painted scene.
- Its functional type is the best fit for the council, which is a task interface.
- The steel palette is the most faithful to the costume.

**Weaknesses**
- The nearest to the gaming and science-fiction look if discipline slips. One glow too many and it is a launcher.
- The least like an invitation: cold, and without the warmth of a hand.
- The figure-forward hero means drawing Doom, which raises the question of closeness to a Marvel-owned design.
- Bodoni's hairlines break at small sizes on phones.
- It shows the least of Latveria as a place.

## Where the boards depart from this document

The boards were built from the specifications above and then adjusted by eye. These are the differences.

- **Direction I.** The eagle perches on a horizontal sceptre gripped in both talons, not holding one upright; it reads cleanly at small sizes and matches the comics' "eagle on a sceptre". Paragraphs in the letter open with a red pilcrow so the gold ruling stays on a fixed line grid. The landscape sheet adds one line: "Sealed for one reader. Break it and it cannot be sealed again."
- **Direction II.** The hero adds a subline: "Victor von Doom, Sovereign of Latveria, will receive you." The throne's side blocks are stepped, to stay further from the film frame. The glyph-green accent is four short lit segments on a floor inlay.
- **Direction III.** The hero adds a sentence: "A summons has been issued in your name. It will open to no one else." The clasp discs carry the square-in-rotated-square and the chain is nearly taut. The rim legend is set in IBM Plex Sans capitals, not mono. A medium-detail ring is used at seal size on the phone plate.

## Questions these boards cannot settle

- **Closeness to Marvel-owned design.** Direction III draws a hooded figure; Direction II stages a small one. Both keep the face a void and avoid the film's clasp emblems, but how close is comfortable is a human decision.
- **The film's release on 18 December 2026.** Direction II depends on it most; Direction I not at all.
- **Tone.** All three boards are played straight. The research notes that Marvel's own parody plays Doom's hospitality for dark comedy, and a movie-planning council among friends may want some of that.
- **The boards are static.** Motion and sound are described, not shown.
