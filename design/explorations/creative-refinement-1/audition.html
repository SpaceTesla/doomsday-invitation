2. The seal waking: now, and with the ticks in turn

When the summons' seal wakes, the app plays a rising hum, three detents as the bands lock, then the runes' ticks. The ticks are meant to run one per rune, 75 ms apart; today all 24 fire in the same instant. The proposal keeps the detents, plays the ticks in turn, and drops the hum.

4. The score: two directions

Both are in A, with a flattened second (B flat) for darkness rather than a minor key, and both are built as layers of the same length (64 s) that start together and loop together. Moving between screens changes which layers are heard and never restarts the music. The opening stays silent: the score begins with GO WITH HIM. The closing screens fade to silence and stay silent.

5. Notes for the decision

  • The layered players use the browser's plain looping, which can leave a tiny gap at the 64-second loop point. The app would cross-fade two copies, so there is no gap there; listen to the journeys for the seamless version.
  • The bed sits about 10 dB under the one-shots, so the detents and the seal stay clear. The drone keeps most of its audible energy above 200 Hz, so a phone speaker plays it.
  • These are prototypes to choose a direction. The chosen one would be refined in a few rounds by ear before it goes anywhere near the app.
