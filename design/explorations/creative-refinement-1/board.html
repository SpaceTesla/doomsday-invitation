1. The council hall

Today the hall is a banner: a 250-pixel strip on a phone, cropped from the wide painting, scrolled away at the first step. The proposal makes the painting the room the council sits in: the upright painting on phones (shipped, never used), the wide one on desktop, held behind every step. The floor circle, the hall's own Doomscript, lights once on arrival and answers the guest's actions. Doom does not move.

What changes

  • The upright hall (hall-p-plate.jpg, hall-p-doom.webp) on phones, the wide one on desktop, fixed behind the steps.
  • A one-time arrival: camera settle, light rising, the ring lighting, the title written, the panel rising. It runs on the first arrival of a visit; reduced motion gets a 0.2 s fade.
  • A response, not a loop: the circle answers approving, claiming a mantle and the decree, once each, about 1.2 s.
  • Principal panels take the summons' brushed steel and four corner bolts. Wells, lists and fields stay as they are.

What stays

  • Doom motionless; no ambient loops; no flicker.
  • Every panel opaque, so text never sits on the painting.
  • The three steps, their behaviour, and every word.
  • On desktop the circle stays visible either side of the column, so the response is seen directly.

Decide

  • Phone: hall first, or content first? Shown here: each step opens on the hall view, with the panel's top at the bottom of the screen, so a guest scrolls once to read it. The alternative opens with the panel at the top and the hall behind; less of the room, no extra scroll. On a phone the panel covers the circle while reading, so there the response is mostly the light washing up the hall.

3. The proposal and the decree

Today the decree is the proposal panel with two words changed. The proposal gives it the weight of an issued act: the mask insignia as issuer, the day as a proclamation, written in place by a passing light, and Doom's signature in runes lighting last. It plays the first time a guest sees a decree, and again after an amendment; a reload shows it settled.

Proposed

Type, within the approved system

  • "Doom decrees" at the council title's size (40 px phone, 52 px desktop) instead of a panel heading's.
  • The day 26 px phone, 34 px desktop; the place 22 / 26 px; both Cormorant Garamond italic, Doom's proclaiming voice. The heading stays the largest line.
  • No new faces, no new colours; the copy is unchanged (8.1, 8.4, 8.5, 8.7).

Once, not on every reload

  • The decree carries a version: finalised, and each amendment. The guest's page records the version it has shown the ceremony for, so a reload, or a later visit, shows the decree settled.
  • Plays again only for a new version (an amendment), with the existing "Doom has amended the decree" line.
  • Where that record lives (the guest's row, like the notices already marked "seen", or the browser) is an implementation choice; the guest's row is the reliable one.

4. The summons: the split plate, and the outer doors

Today the plate's own picture splits and slides apart, over the same plate, blank; the words switch on in one frame as the halves meet; the seal wakes afterwards. In the concept, heavier outer doors stand in front of the real summons. The raised suit rune locks them. The lock releases first; the doors part fully and stay open; a band of light passes across the plate and the words appear in its wake; then the seal wakes, as it does now.
