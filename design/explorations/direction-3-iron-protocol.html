Direction III: The Iron Protocol

The state speaks: Doom as scientist and sovereign at once, a castle reduced to plain stone, and one hairline of emerald light where the machine is.

Hero

The opening screen. A hooded figure stands in a single lancet niche, seen from below, with the ring behind the hood like the rim of a majesty seal. The only colour in the room is light that has a drawn source.

The summons

The decree arrives as an engraved steel plate, the mask's own material. Bright letters are reserved out of a blackened field, a brass strip heads the plate, rivets run each edge, and the ring is the seal.

Identity

Steel is the neutral and emerald is the one colour, always as light. Four typefaces, each with one job.

Motifs

Motion and sound

Described, not shown: this board is static. Five beats move; everything between them holds still.

The five beats

  1. Opening. Black, then the ring is drawn as a line of light, once.
  2. The gate. Hold to be recognised: the ring closes and the seam opens.
  3. The plate. Letters resolve from blur to sharp, line by line. The guest's name lands, then one sheen crosses it.
  4. Entering the council. The plate seam parts like a door.
  5. Approval. A segment of the ring lights and stays lit.

Sound

  • A low drone and the air of a stone room.
  • An iron latch at the gate.
  • A single deep mechanical seat when the plate closes.
  • No beeps and no whooshes. Complete in silence.

Structure

  • The summons advances by tap and hold; each act is also a real button.
  • The council scrolls natively and does not animate while it is read.
  • With reduced motion, every beat becomes a fade.

Strengths and risks

Strengths

  • The most distinct from fantasy, and the only direction that includes Doom the scientist.
  • Portrait-native: the standing figure and the plate both fit a phone without a second composition.
  • The lightest to build: lines, plates and one light. Nothing needs a painted scene.
  • Its functional type is the best fit for the council, which is a task interface.
  • The steel palette is the most faithful to the costume.

Risks

  • The nearest to the gaming and science-fiction look if discipline slips. One glow too many and it is a launcher.
  • The least like an invitation: cold, and without the warmth of a hand.
  • The figure-forward hero means drawing Doom, which raises the question of closeness to a Marvel-owned design.
  • Bodoni's hairlines break at small sizes on phones.
  • It shows the least of Latveria as a place.
