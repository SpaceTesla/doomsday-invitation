IV. The Iron Throne, typography review

The approved typefaces, "Two: Doomsday", set into the opening, the summons and the council. The artwork, palette, wording and positions are those of revision 4. Only the type has changed, with the size changes the new faces need. Revision 4 is kept as it was for comparison.

Approved

  • Pirata One 400 for short display headings.
  • Cinzel 500 for names, titles and figures.
  • Orbitron 600 for short labels in capitals.
  • Cormorant Garamond 500 and italic for body text, 700 for emphasis.
  • The usage rules: no V in Pirata One, no numerals or names in Orbitron, body text at 17 px or more.

Under review on this board

  • Every font size and the hierarchy between them.
  • Regular against italic for body text.
  • How long names are fitted.
  • The council screens, which are a first mock made from approved parts. Their layout and wording are not approved.

Phone frames are drawn at their real size, 390 by 844, so the type in them is the size a guest would see. Desktop frames are scaled to fit the board; "View alone" opens one at the width of your window. Sizes in the tables are given at a 1440 px desktop and a 390 px phone.

What works

  • Pirata One as a title face. "The Iron Throne", "Council of Doom", "Doom proposes" and "The council is seated." all read at once, down to 24 px.
  • Cinzel for names. A name in capitals and small capitals looks engraved, which suits a summons. Accented names set correctly.
  • The opening holds. Both frames keep their composition with nothing moved.
  • Figures in Cinzel sit comfortably inside a line of Cormorant Garamond.
  • Orbitron in small doses. On buttons and tags it gives the technical note without taking over. The council still reads as mostly serif.
  • The phone summons still fits on one screen with a short name, in either treatment.

Where it causes trouble, and what I recommend

#FindingRecommendation
1Regular against italic. A whole summons in italic at 17.6 px is thin and slower to read on a phone. In the council, italic everywhere means nothing marks which words are Doom's. Against that, italic is narrower: it saves two lines on the phone summonsTreatment two. Upright for the body and for explanation. Italic for the opening line, the titling line, "greeting.", the sanction, the dating clause, the hall caption and Doom's proposal
2The summons is tight. Cormorant Garamond needs to be about 15% larger than the earlier face. On the wide plate a short name leaves about 10 px to spare; on the phone, upright, about 10 pxAdopt the tighter phone leading (1.32) and paragraph spacing shown in the proposed fit, for every guest. It makes room for the decline control
3Long names on the summons do not fit as sized. Wide: a two-line name pushes the actions onto the border. Phone: a three-line name runs the actions off the plateWide: when a name takes two lines, name at 85% and body at 2.0%. This fits, with the decline control. Phone: name at 27 px so it takes two lines. Shown above
4One case still does not fit. On the phone, a long name together with the decline control is one line over, about 15 pxDo not shrink the body below 17 px. Let the plate grow by that line and the page scroll for the rare long name. Many phones show less than 844 px of height anyway, so the summons has to survive scrolling
5Long names in the opening take three lines on a phone at 35 px. The text block grows upward over the cuirass. It stays clear of the mask and the handAccept it. Rule: wrap at spaces and hyphens first; step down to 80% only when a single word is too wide; never smaller
6Orbitron is wide and small. Quiet controls sit at 11.5 px, the floor. On the phone panel "Propose another" drops under "Approve"Keep the sizes. Give every quiet control a tap area at least 44 px high. Keep labels to two or three words
7Two lines of capitals. On the summons Doom's name and the guest's name are now the same face in capitals, so they differ only by colour and sizeKeep the guest's name at least 1.25 times Doom's, as here. Do not let the long-name fit bring them level on the wide plate
8Small Cinzel. Holder and proposer names are at 15 px, under the 18 px in the design specification. They read, because they are capitalsAllow 15 px for these secondary names only
9"The Iron Throne" has no place yet. No approved screen carries the title, so it appears here only as a specimenYour call: a title on the first beat of the opening, the browser title and link preview only, or nowhere on screen
