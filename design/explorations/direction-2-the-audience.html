Direction II: The Audience

You are received at court: the first image is Doom's hall, and the summons is handed to you there as an open sheet of parchment under a pool of gold light, with a gilt seal hanging from it.

Hero

The hall as a still. Seven lit steps, a throne that rises out of frame, two shafts of light crossing in the haze, and a small hooded figure who does not get up. The phone version is its own composition, not a crop.

The summons

Letters patent: an open sheet, read in the hall, with the seal hanging from a folded foot. Ink on parchment gives 12.69:1, the strongest reading contrast of the three directions. Gold is the seal and never the text.

Identity

The greens are the olive and sage measured from the film frames, not emerald. Warmth comes from light, and gold is one object.

Motion and sound

Described, not shown: this board is static. Three scenes, advanced by a tap.

The five beats

  1. Opening. The hall resolves out of black, shafts first.
  2. The gate. "Approach the throne." A slow push toward the steps; nothing else moves.
  3. The sheet. It is laid into the light. The first line settles its letterspacing; the guest's name is written in oxblood, then takes one sheen.
  4. Entering the council. Tall doors part.
  5. Approval. The seal swings once and settles.

Sound

  • Stone-hall room tone and a low drone.
  • A single choir swell on the name.
  • A heavy door for the council.
  • Complete in silence: "Enter without sound" is always offered.

What stays still

  • The figure on the throne. He never rises.
  • The letter while it is being read.
  • The council interface.

Strengths and risks

Strengths

  • The most cinematic of the three, and the closest to what guests will have seen in the trailer.
  • The parchment sheet gives the best reading contrast: 12.69:1.
  • The court gives the council a natural setting. You enter a room.
  • Warm and cool light carry meaning without extra ornament.

Risks

  • It needs a painted hall to reach its ceiling, the heaviest artwork of the three. This board draws it in vector.
  • It sits closest to studio-owned design, and details can change when the film opens on 18 December 2026.
  • A wide, symmetrical hall is the hardest composition to keep on a phone.
  • Gold and serif capitals can slide toward the luxury template.
  • The throne room is the image everyone expects.
