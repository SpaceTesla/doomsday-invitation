# Creative Direction v2: The Iron Throne

Phase 1.5, second round. This document specifies the fourth direction and records what stands behind it. The board is `explorations/direction-4-the-iron-throne.html`; open `explorations/index.html` to see it beside the first three.

This is a proposal for approval. It is not the design system, and nothing here has been implemented as a website.

## The direction in one paragraph

Doctor Doom's world is one object seen at three scales. A ring of three bands is the rose window over his throne, the lock that opens between scenes, and the seal on the summons. The outer band is gold and carries his name: sovereignty. The middle band is steel and carries a measured scale: science. The inner band is emerald and carries runes that light only when the mechanism is active: sorcery. The hall and its gold light come from Direction II. The steel plate, the dotted border and the emerald geometry come from Direction III. Doom is drawn as Doom, and appears twice: standing in a doorway when he comes to collect the guest, and seated in the hall.

### What the coverage says about the runes

- The script appeared on Doom-branded coffee packaging and embroidered on the jacket Robert Downey Jr. wore at Comic-Con 2026.
- Fans read it as a one-to-one cipher of the English alphabet with some phonetic spelling. The crib was the packaging, which spells its word "kavi".
- Phrases read on the jacket: "There is no promise of tomorro" (no W), "Justice", "Revenge", "To the victor", "Go the spoils". Two accounts disagree about which pocket carries which word.
- No account says Marvel has named the script or published a key. "Doomscript" and "Doom Cipher" do not appear in the two articles that were read; they are the decoder's own names.
- Letters with no known rune: B, W, X, Y, Z. Popverse also lists Q as unknown; your decoder assigns Q to the hard-C rune.

### What the coverage says about the Banner scene

- Banner is alone in an underground facility, recording a video log.
- The recording stops and the lights go out. He hears someone in the room and asks who it is.
- Doom is described only as a voice. Neither account says he is seen.
- The floor lights with glowing green symbols as Banner begins to change.
- The line is quoted by Moviefone as "Dr. Banner, I'm here to collect you." Your brief gives "Mr. Banner." I have kept your form of address and flag the difference.

## The compositions

### A. The collection

The opening. The page cuts to black. A ring of runes lights on the floor under the guest. A pointed doorway stands open in the dark with the hall behind it, and Doom is already standing in it, backlit, the ring's light on the underside of the mask. His head sits inside the rose window, which reads as a nimbus.

```
DESKTOP                                         PHONE
+---------------------------------------+       +-----------+
|                                /\     |       |    /\     |
|                               /  \    |       |   /()\    |
|  Mr. Anish.                  | () |   |       |  | [] |   |
|  Doctor Doom is here to      | [] |   |       |  | [] |   |
|  collect you.                | [] |   |       |  '----'   |
|  (runes: his name)           |    |   |       | ( runes ) |
|  [Go with him]  without sound'----'   |       | Mr. Anish.|
|      (  ring of runes on the floor  ) |       | line      |
+---------------------------------------+       | [Go with] |
                                                +-----------+
```

- **Copy.** "Mr. Anish." / "Doctor Doom is here to collect you." The action is "Go with him"; the quiet choice is "Go without sound".
- **Why it is not a hero.** There is no headline, no navigation and no background image behind a text block. The name is one of two things in the frame; the other is him.
- **From the scene:** the cut to black, the floor lighting first, his being already present, two short lines.
- **Ours:** the door, the fact that he is seen, the guest answering by choice.

### B. The throne hall

The council chamber, seen from the floor on the centre line. Two tall lancets flank a rose window that is the three-band ring. The throne's back rises to a point under it and carries the ring once more, small. Doom is seated and still. The ring that lit under the guest is inlaid in the floor.

- Emerald appears in exactly two places: the ring above and the ring below.
- About half the frame stays near black. Columns are read by a lit edge.
- On a phone the hall keeps its width at the top and the floor runs on beneath it. That lower area is where council content would sit.

### C. The royal summons

A chamfered steel plate.

- **Edge:** a gold dotted line, a band of engraved runes, a ruled line. The four edges carry the four phrases read on the jacket. Bolts mark the corners.
- **Left:** the mask as a gold line sigil, the seal lit, "Latveria" in runes, and the dating clause.
- **Right:** "Victor von Doom" in gold, the guest's name in silver over one emerald rule, the summons, and "Take your seat".
- **On a phone:** the same plate upright. The sigil gives way to the seal and the date moves into the text. The whole summons fits without scrolling at the size shown.

Wording:

> Victor von Doom, by his own hand and will Sovereign of Latveria, to Mr. Anish, greeting. Be it known that Doom has weighed the defenders of this world and found them wanting; that he now gathers a company of his own choosing; and that you have been chosen. You are therefore summoned to take your seat at the Council of Doom, where the day, the hour and the place shall be set before you. Let none who is called fail to answer. The absent forfeit their voice. Given at Castle Doom, in Doomstadt, the fifteenth day of November, MMXXVI.

### D. The Doomscript identity

The board shows the 22 traced glyphs with the decoder's readings, a table of every inscription used, and four applications: the circular insignia, engraving in metal, the border, and the mechanism in three states.

| Inscription | Standing | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| There is no promise of tomorro | Read by fans on the jacket lapel | Floor ring, top edge of the plate |
| To the victor go the spoils | Read by fans on the jacket cuffs | Inner band of the seal, bottom edge of the plate |
| Justice, Revenge | Read by fans on the jacket pockets | Side edges of the plate |
| Victor von Doom, Latveria | Ours, letter for letter with the decoder; hard C takes the K rune | Outer band of the seal, signature under the greeting |
| Doom | Ours, same terms | Material sample only |

Rules proposed:

1. **Inscription only.** Runes never carry anything the guest must read.
2. **Fixed phrases.** Guest names stay in Latin letters. "Anish" could be spelled; many names could not, and a half-spelled name would be invented.
3. **Light means active.** A rune is engraving until a mechanism turns it on.
4. **No other symbol sets.** No Norse runes, alchemical signs or circuit traces.

Still guesses: whether one rune per letter is right given the phonetic spellings; whether the script has word dividers (the dot between words on the rings is my addition); the two unread runes, which are shown on the board and used nowhere.

The build refuses any text containing a letter the decoder lacks, so nothing on the board contains an invented rune.

### E. The design language (proposed)

**Palette.** Contrast ratios are computed, text colour on Plate `#1A2020`.

| Name | HEX | Job | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night | `#0A0D12` | The guest's world, before the hall | |
| Olive black | `#10130C` | Inside the hall | |
| Plate | `#1A2020` | Every issued object | |
| Gunmetal | `#252C2C` | Raised steel | |
| Dark steel | `#5A6462` | Rules and borders | |
| Steel | `#96A19E` | Secondary text | 6.2:1 |
| Silver | `#D9DEDC` | Text | 12.1:1 (14.3:1 on Night) |
| Emerald deep | `#0E5A40` | Cloth, unlit glass | |
| Emerald | `#1F8F63` | Lines of an active mechanism | 4.1:1, lines only |
| Emerald light | `#4FC395` | Lit runes | 7.5:1 |
| Bronze | `#7D5F2C` | Gold in shadow | |
| Antique gold | `#C6A75E` | His name, dots, bolts | 7.2:1 |
| Gold light | `#E4CB8C` | Window light, large gold type | 10.4:1 |
| Signal | `#E0806F` | Errors only | 5.9:1 |

**Typography.**

- **Bodoni Moda** for names, Doom's lines and headings. Didone faces were cut for steel engraving, the process state documents and banknotes were printed from. That fits an object that is both a decree and a machine.
- **Spectral** for everything read at length. It was drawn for screens and holds on dark grounds.
- **IBM Plex Sans** for controls, counts and labels.
- No monospace and no blackletter.

**Controls.** One primary control: a small steel plate with a dotted inner line and the seal as its icon. The icon is steel at rest and emerald when pointed at, focused or held. Labels say what happens: "Go with him", "Take your seat", "Approve". One quiet control: an underlined text link.

**Panels and borders.** A plate with a dotted inner line for anything Doom issues. A recessed well inside it for counts and lists. The board shows a placeholder ("Doom proposes…") only to show the styling.

**Materials.** Brushed steel, engraving with a lit lower edge, antique gold (never a surface for text), dark stone in large blocks, green wool for hood and cloak only, pale glass as the room's light source.

**Lighting.** Gold shaft from glass. Emerald from below, from active runes. Gold rim on the hood with the face left dark.

**Iconography.** One stroke weight, square ends, no curves but the circle. Every shape comes from the ring, the mask or the armour tile.

### F. Motion and atmosphere

| Beat | What happens | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Doom's arrival | Hard cut to black. A held beat. One rune catches and the ring lights round from it | Cut, 0.4 s hold, about 90 ms a rune |
| 2. The hall opens | A seam of gold widens into the door. He is already in it | 1.6 s, fast start, long settle |
| 3. Emerald illumination | The ring's light climbs him to the underside of the mask. The name appears without movement; the second line follows a pause | 0.6 s, then 0.5 s fade, 0.9 s pause |
| 4. Doomscript wakes | The three bands turn to register at different speeds, no bounce. Runes light in reading order once they lock | Bands 0.7 to 1.1 s; runes 60 to 90 ms |
| 5. The summons is revealed | The plate parts on a centre seam with light in the gap, and closes as one plate bearing the guest's name | 1.2 s open, 0.8 s close |
| 6. Between scenes | The centre of the seal opens like an iris onto the next scene. Back runs it in reverse | 0.9 s |

Rules: machines turn and lock; light switches on and off; nothing floats or bounces. Doom is never animated. One ambient movement is allowed, slow haze in the gold shafts. With reduced motion, each beat is a 0.2 s cross-fade to its end state.

Sound, proposed: the room tone stopping dead at lights-out; a low electrical hum that rises with the ring; dry metal ticks per rune and a heavier detent per band; stone on stone for the door; steel parting for the plate; a large quiet room for the hall. Nothing plays before the first tap, "Go without sound" is always offered, there is no music bed, and no voice is imitated.

## Against Directions II and III

**Retained**

- From II: the hall on one axis, seven gilded steps, gold light through tall glass, a figure who does not move.
- From III: the steel plate, the dotted border, the emerald ring with the nested square, silver type, Bodoni for names.
- From I: the charter order of the wording, the summons as one sealed object.

**Improved**

- **Doom.** II drew a small hooded shape and III a faceless one. IV draws the mask, hood, clasps, chain and cuirass.
- **The hall.** II's was a flat elevation. IV has columns in depth, lancets, a reflecting floor and shafts of light.
- **The ring.** III's carried a Latin legend in monospace. IV's has three bands with a meaning each and is also the room's window.
- **The plate.** Brushed grain, runes on all four edges, corner bolts, the date under the seal.
- **The opening.** III said "Doom has identified you". IV names the guest and says why he has come.
- **Phone.** II lost its figure on a phone. IV's opening is strongest in portrait.

**Discarded**

- II's parchment and gilt seal.
- III's monospace line, coordinates and hold-to-be-recognised gate.
- I's eagle arms, wax seal, night valley and blackletter initial.
- Castoro Titling, Cormorant Garamond, Crimson Pro, IM Fell, Grenze Gotisch, IBM Plex Mono.

## Choices I made that the brief did not specify

- **The ring as the single unifying object.** The brief asked for circles that behave like mechanisms; making one ring serve as window, lock and seal is my proposal.
- **Doom is shown in the opening.** The scene as reported uses only his voice.
- **The clasps carry the nested square**, not the film's emblems, which Phase 1 found reported as other characters' marks.
- **"Mr. Anish" throughout**, including the summons, replacing the earlier placeholder name.
- **The sample panel** ("Doom proposes…") is a styling placeholder, not a feature design.
- **Generator source is saved** in `explorations/assets/board-4-source/` so the board can be rebuilt. It is a drawing script, not application code.

## Limits

- **Drawn, not painted.** No image generator was available. Every scene is original vector drawing, and the materials are approximated with noise and gradients. It is a stand-in for final artwork, not film-quality art.
- **The figure is recognisable but stiff.** It is built from flat planes.
- **Nothing moves or sounds.** Motion is storyboarded in six still frames.
- **Phone frames are simulated** in desktop Chrome at 390 by 844 proportions. Nothing was checked on a device.
- **The Reddit threads were not read.**
- **Closeness to Marvel's design is deliberate**, as you decided. That suits a private invitation among friends and would need review before anything public.

## Decisions needed

1. **Approve Direction IV** as the basis for the design language, or say what to change.
2. **Form of address.** "Mr." needs a title stored per guest. The fallback is the name alone: "Anish."
3. **The second line.** "Doctor Doom is here to collect you" as in your brief, or first person, "I am here to collect you", as in the scene.
4. **Final artwork.** Commission or generate a painted hall and figure, or adopt this flat, graphic style as the look.
5. **Rune rules.** Accept that guest names are never written in runes, and that the word-divider dot is ours.
6. **How close to the costume.** The board follows it closely with our own clasp emblem. Say if it should pull back.
