# Character visual identity research: Avengers: Doomsday

Research for the character emblems: one small mark for each mantle a guest can claim. **This is research only.** No emblem has been drawn, and nothing in the application or the Doom mask has been changed.

- **Written 16 November 2026.**
- **Refined later the same day**, after the owner's brief on design rules. The refinement section below governs. Where a faction entry differs from it, the entry is marked "Revised", and the earlier text is kept for the record.
- **Re-check after release.** Avengers: Doomsday opens on 18 December 2026. Re-check any entry resting on leaked art or toys once it is out.

## How this was researched

- **Builds on the project's existing work:**
  - `research/CREATIVE_FINDINGS.md`, `research/REFERENCE_ANALYSIS.md` and `research/RESEARCH.md`;
  - the notes in `research/research_notes/Doom and Latveria visual research/`;
  - sections 4.5 to 4.7 and 10 of `design/DESIGN.md`.
  
  Doom's visual identity is not researched again here (see faction 7).
- **Three passes:**
  1. A fact-checking sweep: 22 sources and three votes per claim. It confirmed 18 claims and rejected 7.
  2. One researcher per faction, building on the sweep's results.
  3. A targeted refinement pass on the nine uncertain characters and the Fantastic Four. Its evidence is in the appendix.
- **Evidence tiers for the Doomsday looks**, strongest first. Every Doomsday claim names its tier.
  1. Official trailers, posters and stills.
  2. Costume displays at San Diego Comic-Con and D23 2026, Andy Park's concept art, and licensed toys.
  3. The September 2025 crew-gift tote art: leaked, and weak.
  
  When the Doomsday look is unknown, the entry falls back to the latest MCU or Fox film, then the comics.
- **Unopened sources.** Some pages returned 403 or 402 (Marvel.com, the Marvel Fandom wiki, toy retailers). Sources seen only in search results are marked as such.

## The emblem language

**Level 1.** The target is the level 1 form of `web/public/icons/mask-level-1.svg`:
- one filled silhouette in steel (`#d9dedc`);
- its features cut out as negative space: Doom's mask has two eye openings, a mouth slot and two cheek creases;
- no strokes, no shading and no colour;
- readable at 16 px.

Level 2 (gold strokes, rivet dots and inner lines on a dark plate) stays Doom's.

**DESIGN.md 4.7.** The rules for character emblems now live there. They were approved in the owner's brief of 16 November 2026:
- simple curves are allowed;
- ornament stays Doom's;
- no letters, numerals or names appear inside a mark;
- the set is kept clear of Doom's marks.

## Refinement (16 November 2026)

### R1. The curves rule: evaluated and adopted

**What changes.** The old rule is DESIGN.md 4.6: "straight strokes and one circle; the mask is the only form with curves". It kept Doom's mask distinct by being the only curved mark. The research found that 19 of the 38 recommendations need curves, and seven have no straight-lined equivalent: Loki, Sylvie, Wanda, Strange, Deadpool, Magneto and Nightcrawler. Under the old rule those characters would get weaker marks or none.

**What the proposal gets right:**
- It allows simple curves only where they help recognition.
- It keeps ornament for Doom.
- It holds every emblem to Level 1.

**What it leaves open.** The mask at level 1 has no ornament: it is a smooth filled face. Once characters may curve, curves stop being what sets Doom apart, so something else has to. DESIGN.md 4.7 now records four safeguards:
1. **No borrowing from Doom.** No emblem takes the mask's outline, a hood over a faceplate, eye slots shaped like his, the riveted plate, the clasps, the horned mark, the cuirass tile, the nested square, the seal or Doomscript.
2. **Emblems are steel.** They are never gold and never drawn at level 2. The only emerald is the claimed state that section 8.6 already defines.
3. **Doom leads.** Where emblems and the mask appear together, the mask comes first and is never smaller.
4. **No lit eyes.**

**What "simple curves" means.** Each curve is a single sweep or a part of a circle. There are no scrolls, filigree, hatching or texture fields. Aim for one to three cut-outs, and never more than the mask's five.

**Scope.** The rule now applies to character emblems only. The project's own marks (Doom's, Latveria's and the interface's) keep the old 4.6 rule unchanged. **Adopted.**

### R2. The typography rule: adopted

No emblem contains a letter, a numeral, a name or any lettering. The name is always set beside the mark in Cinzel. This touched:
- **Fantastic Four:** the numeral 4 is replaced by a shared quatrefoil (R3.9).
- **Sentry:** the S is replaced by the eclipsed sun (R3.5).
- **Mystique:** the comics belt carries an X, so the refinement uses her skull diadem instead.
- **Rejected earlier on the same grounds:** Steve's helmet "A" and the X-Men "X". No other recommendation used lettering.

The typography system itself is unchanged.

### R3. The uncertain recommendations, revisited

**R3.1 U.S. Agent: his bent shield (Medium-high, up from Low).**
- **The evidence.** In *Thunderbolts\**, Sentry bends Walker's shield "like a taco", and it stays bent in the post-credits scene and in the August 2025 still. The Direct (22 October 2026) reports that the Doomsday trailer and every promo still show it bent; only Marvel Snap card art shows it repaired.
- **Correction.** This reverses the first pass's assumption that Doomsday shows it fixed.
- **Why it works.** A folded disc seen side-on has an outline no Captain America shield has. Arcs of the rings keep it read as a shield, with no star, so it never reads as Cap.
- **Alternative.** Three medals on a bar, with plain pendants and no stars. Walker won three Medals of Honor and welded one to his homemade shield. It is unique, but weaker at 16 px.
- **Rejected:**
  - the blood-smeared shield, which needs colour and is still a disc;
  - the comics chest design, whose offset star competes with Steve's mark.

**R3.2 Ghost: a faceless figure and its banded phantom copy (Medium-low, up from Low).**
- **What sources don't show.** None describes a chest emitter, a core or a shoulder piece, and whether her *Thunderbolts\** suit keeps the hood is unverified. Her one non-phasing feature, the faceplate under a hood, is the shape that echoes Doom.
- **What is well documented is the phasing.** The 2018 visual-effects team describe "phantom images", and the *Thunderbolts\** team describe offset, streaked "leaves".
- **The emblem.** An upright, faceless figure with a sideways-shifted copy cut into horizontal bands. It is a new shape family on the roster: the only full figure, with no face and no hood.
- **Why the confidence is only medium-low.** A banded echo at 16 px needs testing; it could read as a generic "glitch".
- **Alternative.** A figure half through a wall.

**R3.3 Namora: the lionfish-fin crest, made asymmetric and webbed (Medium, up from Medium-low).**
- **The association is official.** Ruth E. Carter says her headdress "echoes the fins of a lionfish". Disney's D23 guide describes "a collar and headdress inspired by a lionfish" and "a complex, spiky collar".
- **The refinement.** Sweep the crest back to one side and web the spines together. That keeps it from reading as a symmetric crown (Loki, Wanda) or as a ray burst (Doom's rayed clasp, Sentry's sun).
- **Caveats:**
  - lionfish spines were used across Talokan headdresses generally (TheWrap);
  - her red feathers and spear are shared with other Talokan;
  - her Doomsday costume is unseen (a brief appearance in teaser 4);
  - the blue stone is unverified.
- **Alternative.** A whole lionfish in profile.

**R3.4 Steve Rogers: the round shield with Mjolnir crossed behind it (Medium-high, up from Medium).**
- **Why it's his.** The *Endgame* moment when Steve wields both shield and hammer is the most cited audience moment found for him. It is his alone: no one else has carried both. It also matches Doomsday, where he wields Mjolnir and the trailer ends on him lifting it.
- **Why not the triangular shield.** In *The First Avenger* it is a decorative stage-tour shield that gets dented and replaced, and no evidence was found that casual viewers recognise it.
- **Why the plain round shield is free again.** U.S. Agent has moved to the bent shield and Peggy Carter has a hat, so only the two Captains still use a shield. They differ by what breaks the rim: wings for Sam, the hammer for Steve.
- **Drawing constraints.** The hammer breaks out past the rim on a diagonal and never sits inside the disc. Keep Thor's Stormbreaker axe-led so the two hammers do not echo.
- **The clasp.** Doom's knot clasp is a squared knot. The press reads it as Mjolnir, but it is not drawn as a hammer, so the clash is conceptual, not visual.
- **Alternative.** The triangular shield. The compass is unreadable at 16 px.

**R3.5 Sentry: a half-eclipsed sun (Medium).**
- **Why the duality.** *Thunderbolts\** is built on Sentry and his dark side, the Void:
  - the VFX supervisor treated the Void as the city's only light source, casting long radial shadows;
  - the posters sold the black Void silhouette;
  - co-creator Paul Jenkins describes the character as "the bit that's light, the bit that's dark".
- **Why not a plain sunburst.** It would read as Doom's rayed clasp, or as the seal's dotted ring.
- **The emblem.** Rays on one half only, and a large circular "shadow bite" taken out of the other half, leaving a thin rim. That makes the mark his.
- **Unverified.** A golden glow on screen.
- **Alternative.** A black sun with uneven shadow streaks.

**R3.6 Shang-Chi: three rings, three-quarter view, stacked on a diagonal (Medium).**
- **Ten hoops in a circle** shrink at 16 px into a dotted ring, the project's own seal mark.
- **Rings on a forearm** collide with the Winter Soldier's arm.
- **The Great Protector dragon** is illegible at that size.
- **Three thick ellipses, overlapping on a diagonal with no arm,** are unlike anything else on the roster. Their three openings are the cut-outs.
- **Unconfirmed.** His Doomsday suit is not described in any source that could be opened, and the "five rings per arm" detail comes from one article's own narration, not from the filmmakers.

**R3.7 Mystique: the skull diadem (Medium).**
- **Her Doomsday look.** The trailer and the D23 display confirm a white suit with long sleeves, a high collar and a small tiara. Two outlets report a skull headpiece in the trailer, matching a 2025 leak.
- **What changes.** The skull now hangs small from a thin band, so it reads as jewellery, not as a face. That takes Mystique out of the front-facing faces and away from Doom's mask.
- **What is out.** Her belt carries an X, which the typography rule excludes.
- **Watch.** This joins the headpiece group (R4.3).
- **Alternative.** Her head in profile, wearing the band, with scale notches.

**R3.8 Scarlet Witch: the crown, kept and made specific (Medium).**
- **The shape.** An open crown that frames the face:
  - two short top points;
  - a centre point dropping to the brow;
  - side arms ending at the cheekbones.
  
  Cut-outs: the face opening and a small teardrop jewel.
- **Why it stays distinct.** Its downward tips separate it from Loki, Sylvie and Namora, whose points rise. The open frame, with no dome, separates it from Magneto's helmet.
- **Sources.** The cheekbone length rests on one trade article (The Direct) and Andy Park's concept-art variants.

**R3.9 The Fantastic Four: a quatrefoil family (Medium for each).**
- **No existing symbol to draw on.** First Steps shows no non-numeral team symbol. The closest comics precedent is the 1963 Fantasticar, which "could separate into four distinct modules". So the shared device is our own: a heraldic quatrefoil, four lobes around a solid centre, which suits the project's heraldic language.
- **Rules for the base:**
  - the lobes are mirror-symmetric, so no version can read as a swastika;
  - the centre is solid, so the gaps never form a cross, which the press reads as a Mjolnir device;
  - no square sits at the centre, which would echo the seal's nested square.
- **Each member changes the whole outline in one way:**
  - **Reed:** one lobe drawn out into a long, narrowing reach. An uneven stretch reads as stretching better than an even one.
  - **Sue:** solid on one side, dissolving into vertical slats on the other. The slats must not look like a barcode.
  - **Johnny:** each lobe rises into a flame tip. The flames all point up, never leaning one way, so they never form a pinwheel.
  - **Ben:** the lobes are recut as faceted rock, with two or three cracks. The facets stay irregular, so they don't echo the suit rune's hexagon.
- **The cost.** The group loses the instant "4", so the faction heading and the names carry more of the identification. That is why each sits at Medium rather than the earlier High.
- **The gain.** All four leave the disc family.

**R4.1 Discs.**

**R4.2 Faces from the front.**
- **Before:** eleven, plus Doom.
- **Now, seven plus Doom:** Ant-Man, Red Guardian, Magneto, Deadpool, Shuri, M'Baku and Hulk. In each, the outline carries the identity: antennae, cannonball dome, crest and cheek guards, eye patches, short wide ears, crown crest, shoulders.

**R4.3 Headpieces: the largest remaining group, now five.**

## 1. The Avengers (Earth-616)

Shared finding for this faction: in Doomsday, **Steve Rogers carries Mjolnir and no shield**, and **Thor leads with Stormbreaker**. So the shield cannot separate the two Captains (Sam carries the same round vibranium shield Steve handed over in Endgame), and Mjolnir cannot be Thor's emblem (Steve holds it, and the press already reads one of Doom's cloak clasps as a Mjolnir device). The split proposed below is: Sam = round shield with wings; Steve = the old heater shield; Thor = Stormbreaker; Torres = wings with no shield.

Correction to the focus brief: Torres's Falcon kit is **green and grey with a full helmet and a yellow visor**, not red and white. Red-and-white wings with goggles were Sam's old Falcon kit. Colour cannot carry identity in a monochrome emblem anyway, so the shapes have to separate them.

### Character: Captain America / Sam Wilson

**1. Relevant incarnation**
Doomsday. Sam wears his Captain America: Brave New World final-battle suit. The earlier pass confirmed this (single secondary source, The Direct). ### Faction note: one family of "4"s, not four unrelated marks

**What Doomsday shows (as of 16 November 2026).** The team keeps the blue-and-white First Steps bodysuits and adds new jackets. The Thing gets no jacket.

- Crew-gift art, September and October 2025 (weak tier): Reed wears a white, lab-coat-like jacket with a blue collar. Sue's jacket has colours "opposite" to Reed's and may be a gilet. Johnny's jacket is blue. Ben has no new jacket (The Direct, opened).
- SDCC 2026 display: "Vanessa Kirby's Sue Storm FF uniform" was shown. No detail was published (Comics Beat, opened).
- World Series promo, 24 October 2026: Reed pitches at Ebbets Field on Earth-828 in a baseball uniform numbered 4, with one arm stretched out (Comic Basics, opened). The source has not been confirmed as official.

So nothing shows a new team emblem. The First Steps chest mark is a blue 4 on a white disc with a blue-and-white border (Den of Geek, opened). It is still the team's one constant.

**Recommendation: a family of four variations on the 4.** Each emblem is a filled steel disc with a 4 cut out of it, a plain sans-serif numeral that we draw ourselves and do not trace from the studio logo. Each character then changes the disc's silhouette in one way:
- Reed: the disc is pulled out sideways.
- Sue: the disc fades out in slats.
- Johnny: the disc burns.
- Ben: the disc is cut into stone.

Why this beats four unrelated emblems:
1. The 4 makes all four read as one faction at once. The roster groups mantles by faction, so that matters.
2. At 16 px the outlines still differ: a wide bar, a striped disc, a flame and a faceted polygon.
3. A 4 is straight strokes, and the disc is the "one circle", so three of the four fit the icon rules. Only Johnny's flame needs curves.
4. The unrelated set collides with the roster. A fist reads as Hulk. A bubble or round shield reads as Captain America's shield, Shang-Chi's rings or Strange's mandalas. A stretching arm has no strong outline of its own.

Risks for the owner:
- (a) Is a numeral inside a mark a "figure" under the typography rule that all figures are set in Cinzel? The 4 should be drawn as a mark, not set in a typeface. The owner should confirm.
- (b) A disc with a cut-out sits near the site's own seal mark, a circle around a nested square. The seal has no numeral, so the risk is small.
- (c) If the owner wants each emblem to stand alone without the faction context, use the alternatives listed for each character.

Keep the spines long, thin, uneven and webbed at the base, not even sun-rays. Keep them straight, unlike flames. Keep them many and fanned, unlike two horns.

Draw the legs as straight segments with one angled knee each, so no curves are needed, except perhaps a slight taper on the abdomen. This fits the straight-stroke rule better than most character marks. Honest limit: at 16 px only the proportions say "Raimi" rather than "any Spider-Man". The roster has only one Spider-Man, so that is acceptable.
- *Distinct from the roster:* No other spider on the roster. Keep the legs thin and the body narrow so it does not read as a beetle or star. There are no known collisions.

### Keeping the three magic users apart

The three sit in different geometric families:

| Character | Element | Geometry |
|---|---|---|
| Wanda | Crown | A head ornament: a wide band with points rising upwards |
| Clea | Blade in a jagged rift | Vertical and angular, straight strokes only |

- Wanda never gets a circle.
- Clea never gets an almond or a crown.
- Strange never gets points.

## Cross-character consistency analysis

### 2. Pairs and groups that need deliberate separation

**The two Captain Americas.** Sam carries the very round shield Steve handed him in *Endgame*, so the shield on its own cannot tell them apart. Steve, meanwhile, appears in Doomsday out of uniform, holding Mjolnir and carrying no shield. The evidence:
- Andy Park's concept art (tier 2);
- the Hot Toys Doomsday figure, October 2026 (tier 2);
- press accounts of the trailer's last shot (tier 1, at second hand).

The separation that holds is:
- **Sam: the round shield with wings.** The wings say Sam; the disc says Captain America.
- **Steve: the triangular shield from *The First Avenger*.** Its outline differs entirely from a disc.

Both carry a star. That is acceptable because the outlines differ, but the star should stay small so it does not read as the press's "Captain Marvel" star on Doom's clasp. A risk remains: Steve's mark draws on his 1940s film, not on Doomsday. If he suits up on screen, re-check it.

**Sam, Joaquin Torres and Namor (wings).**
- Sam's wings spread level behind a disc.
- Torres's sweep up into a V and meet at a small diamond, with no disc and no star.
- Namor's is one small wing on a foot and must never appear without the foot.
- Torres's Brave New World suit is green and grey with a full helmet and yellow visor. The red-and-white goggled kit was Sam's old Falcon suit. Colour doesn't survive Level 1 anyway, so the shape is what separates them.

**Steve, Sam, U.S. Agent, Red Guardian and Winter Soldier (stars).**
- The faction research already avoids a star used on its own anywhere: the Winter Soldier's mark drops it (his vibranium arm has none), U.S. Agent's shield is starless, and Red Guardian's star sits small inside a helmet outline.
- Only the two Captains carry a star as a main feature, and their outlines differ.

**Loki, Sylvie, Scarlet Witch and Namora (points on the head).** Each is separated by count and symmetry:
- **Loki:** two whole, tall, symmetrical horns. Never crack or break them, though the series finale's horns were cracked.
- **Sylvie:** lopsided, one horn and a jagged stub. Her costume designer broke the horn deliberately (Christine Wada, Looper). Which side is broken still needs a screen frame.
- **Scarlet Witch:** a drop point on the forehead and two *short* upswept points. It reads as a crown, not as horns.
- **Namora:** five to seven *straight, uneven* spines joined at the base. Unevenness also keeps her clear of Doom's rayed clasp.

**Doctor Strange, Clea and Scarlet Witch (magic).** The faction 7 research gives each a separate shape family, and this review agrees:
- **Strange:** round and rotational (the Vishanti and Sanctum window). No points.
- **Clea:** straight lines in a vertical shape (a dagger in a jagged rift). Never an almond, which would read as the Eye of Agamotto, and never a crown.
- **Wanda:** upward points (the crown). No circle.
- None of the three is a hand, which also keeps them clear of Doom's raised hand.

**The Thing, Hulk, Winter Soldier and Wolverine (fists).**
- The Thing's mark is the stone 4, not a fist, and Hulk's is a bust, not a fist. That leaves two fists.
- Keep the Winter Soldier's as an upright forearm with plate seams and no blades.
- Keep Wolverine's as a sideways knuckle block with three long blades. The blades dominate.

**Nightcrawler and Gambit (card suits).** Nightcrawler's tail ends in a spade, so Gambit's card must carry a **diamond** pip, never a spade. The card's rectangle also sits near the project's cuirass tile and nested square. The zigzag energy edge and the tilt keep it apart.

**Mobius, Loki and Sylvie (the TVA).** The TVA logo, TemPad and time door belong to all three, and Loki even holds a TVA card in the trailer. So Mobius gets the jet ski, the only object that is his alone. It is an in-joke, so it relies on his name being shown beside it, as the council already does.

**Peggy Carter and the shields.** The original Peggy is confirmed, not Captain Carter. Her hat keeps her out of the shield family entirely. If a Captain Carter reveal lands before release, revisit her mark.

**Shuri and Wolverine; M'Baku, Beast and Hulk (animal and beast heads).**
- Shuri's panther ears must stay short and wide, so they don't read as Wolverine's cowl fins. Wolverine's mark is the claws, not the cowl, which already removes most of the clash.
- M'Baku's crown crest and a jaw wider than his brow separate the gorilla from Beast's swept mane and from Hulk's shoulders-first bust.

### 3. Adjustments recommended by this review

These are this review's own synthesis, not findings from the sources.

**A. Thin out the discs.**
- The disc belongs to the Fantastic Four as a family: it is their logo, and each of the four changes the outline in a different way.
- Outside the four, keep the disc only where it is the character's object: Sam's shield, Strange's window, and Shang-Chi's broken ring of hoops.
- **Yelena:** use the hourglass on its own, two triangles point to point, with no disc. This is her own listed alternative. The hourglass reads as "Black Widow" generally, but Natasha is not on the roster.
- **Sentry:** use a bare angular S, or an S on a squared plate, with no disc. Avoid setting it in a diamond, which reads as Superman.

**B. U.S. Agent is the weakest mark on the roster.**
- A starless ringed disc reads, at 16 px, as Captain America's shield with the star missing.
- The film shield itself is unconfirmed: sources give sculpted rings with some red, and separately a black shield with no blue field.
- Before design, confirm the on-screen shield. If it stays a ringed disc, consider his listed alternative: the bent "taco" shield, which is unmistakably his for anyone who has seen *Thunderbolts\**. Doomsday art reportedly shows the shield repaired, so this alternative trades accuracy for recognisability, and that is the owner's call.

**C. Cut the number of faces, or make each outline carry its identity.**
- Twelve faces sit beside Doom's mask. A face earns its place only where its outline is the identifying feature on its own: Ant-Man's antennae, Magneto's crest and cheek guards, Deadpool's eye patches, Shuri's ears, Professor X's Cerebro ribs, M'Baku's crest, and Hulk's shoulders.
- The weakest faces are the first to test against an alternative:
  - **Ghost** (Low): her faceplate and hood echo Doom's mask and hood. Her alternative, "phasing alone", is a shape broken into offset bands.
  - **Mystique**: no film source shows the skull, and it reads as a generic "death" sign. Her alternatives are a split face with scales, or a segment of the skull belt.
  - **Red Guardian**: his brow star on the film helmet is unconfirmed.
- No face may use eye openings shaped like Doom's: wide, flat slots that taper outwards. Doom's eyes are also never shown lit (DESIGN.md 10), so no emblem gets glowing eyes.

**D. What no emblem may borrow from Doom.** Faction 7 covers this in full:
- the mask outline, the hood and the riveted plate;
- the emerald, which is reserved for Doom's own marks;
- the rayed and knot clasps;
- the horned mark, which is already set aside in DESIGN.md 4.5.

### 4. Decisions the owner must make before design

These come out of the research, but they are product and design calls, not research findings.

1. **The curves rule.** DESIGN.md 4.6 reserves curves for Doom's mask. Of the 38 recommendations, 19 need curves and three more (Sue, Beast and Namora) need one or two circles. Sixteen can be drawn in straight strokes: Steve, Thor, Torres, Yelena and Sentry (once adjustment A drops their discs), Winter Soldier, Reed (nearly), The Thing, Cyclops, Gambit, Wolverine, M'Baku, Spider-Man, Mobius, Hulk and Clea. There are two ways forward:
   - **Exempt character emblems from the rule.** Doom's mask stays the most important mark by its size, its placement and its colour role.
   - **Keep the rule.** Swap curved recommendations for straight-lined alternatives wherever one exists: the Thing's polygon, the knobkerrie, the spear, the stepped feathers. Loki, Sylvie, Wanda, Strange, Deadpool, Magneto and Nightcrawler have no straight-lined equivalent.
2. **Letters and numerals in marks.** The Fantastic Four's 4 and Sentry's S put a numeral and a letter inside an emblem. The type rules set "all figures in Cinzel", and the suit rune is "a mark and never a letter". The faction research suggests drawing the 4 and the S as part of the mark, not setting them in a typeface. That needs agreeing.
3. **Colour.** DESIGN.md gives colours by role: gold when Doom issues, emerald when something is active, steel otherwise. Character emblems would be steel at rest. The council's existing "yours" state (an emerald border with the mark in emerald light) already covers a claimed mantle. No character colour is needed.
4. **Source and approval.** DESIGN.md 10 forbids new images of any character without approval and forbids studio logos. These emblems would be redrawn interpretations of costume elements, never traces of official logos (the FF 4, Sentry's S, the X-Men X, Deadpool's belt). That needs the owner's approval to begin.
5. **The "Latverian Witches".** If Scarlet Witch, Clea or Sylvie appear in Doomsday wearing Doom-styled masks, as the SDCC 2026 costumes suggest to some fans, the faction research recommends ignoring that look for their emblems. Otherwise they would read as Doom.

##### Does the "lionfish-spine headdress" recommendation hold?
**Yes on the evidence, with one caveat about the shape.**
- **What holds:** both Carter and Disney's own guide tie the lionfish to Namora by name, and Disney adds a spiky lionfish collar.
- **Caveat 1, the family it sits in:** drawn as a symmetric fan on a brow band, it joins the crowded "points rising from a headband" group (Loki, Sylvie, Scarlet Witch). It also sits near Doom's rayed clasp.
- **Caveat 2, generic Talokan:** TheWrap says lionfish spines were used in Talokan headdresses generally.
- **What separates it:** the lionfish fin's signature is long, separate spines joined by webbing partway up. That should drive the drawing, not crown symmetry.

##### Candidates that are hers, not Namor's or generic Talokan
1. **A lionfish-fin crest:** the headdress drawn as one swept-back, webbed fan of spines.
2. **The lionfish itself, in profile:** her designer's animal. Namor's material is serpent, feather and Maya imagery; Attuma's is the hammerhead.
3. **The spiked lionfish collar:** a ring of spikes round the neck. **Rejected:** a radial ring reads as a sunburst and collides with Doom's rayed clasp.

##### Recommended primary: the lionfish-fin crest, swept back and asymmetric
- **Level 1 silhouette:** a short curved brow band at the base. From it rise five or six long, thin, straight spines of unequal length, all leaning back in one direction, like a lionfish dorsal fin.
- **Webbing:** solid across the lower third to half of the spines, with the free tips separated. The lean and the webbing keep it from reading as a symmetric crown or a set of radial rays.
- **Cut-outs:** the V-gaps between the free spine tips (two or three deep notches); one small round stone on the band (optional, because the blue stone is unverified); one curved slit where the webbing meets the band.
- **Curves:** the band and the webbing edge.

**Alternative: a lionfish in profile.**
- **Silhouette:** a compact body with a tall fanned dorsal crest of separate spines and one large wing-like pectoral fan sweeping back and down.
- **Cut-outs:** a round eye, a gill slit, and the gaps between the dorsal spines.
- **Trade-off:** the most unmistakably "lionfish" option and outside every crowded family, but more "fish" than "warrior".

**Confidence: Medium.**
- **For:** the lionfish link is firm and official (designer plus Disney).
- **Against:** the Doomsday costume is unknown, the comic look is unverified, and lionfish spines are partly a general Talokan material.

- **Cut-outs:**
  1. a thin vertical sliver separating the solid figure from the copy;
  2. two or three horizontal gaps that break the copy into bands, standing for the streaks and the translucency;
  3. optionally, one horizontal glitch slice through the solid figure, with that band nudged a pixel or two.
- **Curves:** the head and shoulders.
- **Why it reads as Ghost:** two films and two VFX houses built her identity on offset copies of her body, the "phantom images" and the "leaves". Nobody else on the roster has an after-image.
- **Why it is safe:** a whole figure is a new outline family in this roster (no other mark is a full figure). It has no face, no mask and no hood, so it cannot echo Doom.

**Alternative: phasing through a wall.**
- **Silhouette:** a tall vertical slab with a figure (same plain head) stepping through it. The leading half stands solid on one side of the slab; the trailing half shows only as banded slices on the other.
- **Cut-outs:** the slab edges where the body crosses (a negative outline), plus the band gaps.
- **Trade-off:** clearer about intangibility, but busier at 16 px.

**Rejected:**
- the hood, the faceplate and the glitch-sliced faceplate: too close to Doom's mask and hood;
- the helmet side lights: a detail, not a silhouette;
- the New Avengers shoulder logo: it reads as the Avengers "A", and it is a letter;
- a chest core: unverified.

**Confidence: Medium for the concept** (phasing is the strongest documented and most consistent Ghost signal); **Low-Medium for 16 px legibility.** A two-figure mark at 16 px depends on a clean offset, so it needs a pixel test.

- **Leak (November 2025, @variablelace):** "white dress with blue accents on the shoulders and [the] center of chest, white pants under suit", an X on the belt and "the tiara with the skull". https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-doomsday/avengers-doomsday-will-see-doctor-doom-wield-a-spoiler-costume-descriptions-for-shang-chi-and-mystique-a224868 and https://www.superherohype.com/movies/639264-avengers-doomsday-update-gives-new-details-on-mcu-x-men-costumes (both opened; 14 November 2025)

**Summary of the Doomsday evidence**
- **Confirmed (trailer, display):** the white suit, long sleeves, the small tiara or headpiece, blue skin.
- **Strongly indicated:** the scales (one trailer report).
- **Reported from the trailer by two outlets, and matching the leak:** the headpiece carries a skull. D23 write-ups call it only a "small tiara".
- **The belt:** an X belt (display report and leak). That is the X-Men insignia, letter-shaped, generic to the team and against the no-letters rule, so it is unusable.
- **The skull belt:** not reported for the film.

**The comics.** Dave Cockrum's costume: "a belt of golden skulls, which were essentially pouches". The skull-pendant headband "is a feature of most of her costumes" (stated for the Age of Apocalypse and other variants). https://www.cbr.com/x-men-best-mystique-costumes/ (opened)

##### Weighing the concepts
- **Skull headband or diadem.** Now supported by the comics (a constant across most costumes) and, at second hand, by the Doomsday trailer.
  - **Risk:** a skull is face-shaped, and it can read as a generic "death" sign.
  - **Fix:** draw it as a band with a small skull, so it reads as jewellery, not a head.
- **Skull belt segment:** comic-only, unreported for the film, unreadable under 32 px.
- **Split face with scales:** front-facing, abstract, adds a face. Reject.
- **Head in profile with scale texture:** not front-facing, and the scales are now supported for Doomsday.
  - **Risk 1:** a profile head reads as a generic woman.
  - **Risk 2:** scales also point to Namor's Doomsday scale tunic.
  - **Fix:** it works only if the skull diadem is visible on the brow.
- **Better ideas from Doomsday material:** none. The suit's own graphics are the X belt (unusable), the blue accents (colour) and cutouts (too fine).

##### Recommended primary: the skull diadem, a band with a hanging skull
- **Level 1 silhouette:** a thin, gently arched headband running nearly the full width. Hanging from its centre is a small skull, about 40% of the mark's height: a rounded cranium, a pinched jaw, and short side tabs where it joins the band.
- **The skull is an ornament on a band, not a head.** The wide thin arc dominates the outline.
- **Cut-outs:** two round eye sockets (never Doom's long tapered slots), one small inverted-triangle nose notch, and optionally a single short tooth slot.
- **Curves:** the band and the cranium.
- **Separation from Scarlet Witch's crown (a forehead drop):** Mystique's band is thin and flat-ended, with no upswept points, and the drop is a skull, not a point.
- **Why:** it is the only motif present in both her comic history and the Doomsday trailer reports, and it carries no colour or letter.

**Alternative: the head in profile wearing the skull diadem, with scales.**
- **Silhouette:** a side-view head and neck, slicked short hair at the back, the diadem's skull at the brow.
- **Cut-outs:** two or three scale-shaped notches along the cheek and neck, and an eye slit.
- **Trade-off:** not front-facing and shows both signatures, but busier at 16 px.

**Confidence: Medium (up from the earlier Medium, which rested on comics alone).**
- **For:** the skull is now reported from the Doomsday trailer.
- **Against:** the D23 write-ups describe only a "small tiara", and no official still I could open shows the ornament's exact shape. Check a trailer frame or the film before final drawing.

- Co-creator and film consultant Paul Jenkins (AIPT, opened): "The Sentry is about the two sides of ourselves … the bit that's light, the bit that's dark". **Golden radiance.**

(search only): https://looper.com/1365245/marvel-teases-a-huge-change-to-its-most-powerful-hero
- On-screen golden glow: **not verified.** Neither VFX interview I opened describes a golden glow. Morrison mentions only a telekinesis "hummingbird" effect and force bubbles.

**Costume details other than the S.**

- Cape colour is still unverified (gold or blue).
- **No sun, ray or chest motif other than the S was found.** The costume gives no non-letter mark.

##### Assessment: plain sunburst against eclipse

- **A sunburst alone** is generic (it could be any sun or star hero). Worse, a disc ringed with even rays is essentially Doom's rayed clasp. Short, evenly spaced rays also start to read as the seal's dotted ring at 16 px. **Reject.**
- **An eclipsed sun** carries what Thunderbolts* actually puts on screen. The film's climax is the Void. The marketing sold the black silhouette. The VFX treated the Void as an inverted sun casting radial shadow. The co-creator frames the character as light against dark. The duality is not a side note: it is the plot (Bob, the Sentry and the Void are one person). So the eclipse is his. It also solves the Doom collision: rays on only half the disc break the full-circle "rayed disc" read.

##### Recommendation

**Primary: the half-eclipsed sun.**
- Silhouette: one filled disc with 4-5 short, straight, blunt rays fanning from its *left/upper half only*. The right half has no rays, and its edge stays smooth.
- Cut-out 1: a large circular "bite", the Void's shadow, offset to the lower right. It removes about 40% of the disc and leaves a thick crescent of sun plus a thin rim (at least 2 px at 16 px) closing the dark side. The disc then reads as a sun with its own darkness inside it, not as a crescent moon.
- Cut-out 2 (optional): one narrow slot between two rays to keep them from merging at 16 px.
- Asymmetry is the identity. Never put rays all the way round (Doom's clasp), and never use evenly spaced stubs (the seal's dotted ring).

**Alternative: the "black sun".** A solid disc (the Void) with 5-6 long, tapering, *uneven* shadow streaks radiating from its lower half, as in the VFX "very long shadows". Cut-out: two small eye slits in the disc (the poster's white eyes). It is darker and more Void-forward, but it drifts toward a face and toward Namora's spines.

**Confidence: Medium.** The duality is strongly sourced (VFX, posters, Jenkins). The golden glow on screen is not. A crescent-like bite risks a "moon" read, so the rim and the rays must carry "sun".

##### Assessment at 16 px

| Concept | 16 px read | Collision |
|---|---|---|
| Ten hoops in a circle | Each hoop gets about 2 px, so it collapses into a beaded ring or a gear | **Seal's dotted ring** (direct hit); Doom's rayed disc |
| Bangles on a forearm (five bands) | Arm plus horizontal slots | **Winter Soldier** (a forearm with horizontal seam slits): direct hit |
| Dragon head | A blob; it needs many curves and fine detail | Low, but illegible; also the Ta Lo dragon is not his signature |
| New Doomsday suit | Nothing verifiable to draw | None |

##### Recommendation

**Primary: three rings sliding off the arm, with no arm drawn.**
- Silhouette: three thick hoops seen in three-quarter view (ellipses), stacked on a short diagonal with each overlapping the next. This is the bracer caught as it flies off the forearm.
- Cut-outs: the three inner elliptical openings. That is exactly three, and they read as "rings".
- Why: it keeps the on-screen fact (bangles worn and thrown) without the forearm that would collide with the Winter Soldier, and without the ten-dot circle that collides with the seal.
- The ellipses keep it clear of Sam's front-on disc and of every flat ring on the roster. Don't count to ten: the shape says "the rings", not "ten".
- Watch-out: if the stack is too regular, it reads as a coil spring. Offset it on a diagonal and vary the overlap slightly.

**Alternative: a single large ring with a short, curled dragon tail through it.** It joins the two Ta Lo/Ten Rings halves of his story and is more distinctive, but it is busier.

**Confidence: Medium.** The rings are certain, but the band profile and count on screen rest on an author's narration, not a filmmaker. The Doomsday suit offers nothing.

##### Evidence

**Doomsday.**
- The trailer (dropped Monday 20 July 2026) ends with Steve catching or summoning Mjolnir. "Sam has the shield, but the hammer just changed the equation" (opened). https://cosmicbook.news/avengers-doomsday-official-trailer

**(b) The round shield alone.**

**(c) Shield plus Mjolnir.** Endgame's "worthy" moment is the most-cited MCU audience moment I found:

The Doomsday trailer's last shot repeats it, and press framed it as "still worthy". The pairing is therefore both his past peak and his Doomsday present. I could not find a source stating that Steve is the *only* character shown wielding both at once, though I know of no counter-example. Treat that as unverified.

**(d) The compass with Peggy's photo.** It is emotionally central (*The First Avenger* crash; Endgame trailer and ending). Coverage is fan or trailer analysis, with no official statement (search only): https://www.themarysue.com/steve-rogers-and-the-sad-story-about-that-compass/, https://comicbook.com/2018/12/07/avengers-endgame-black-widow-really-holding-captain-americas-compass/ At 16 px it is a small round case, read as a pocket watch. **Collision check for (c).**
- Doom's left clasp is a steel disc carrying a squared knot that the press reads as a "logo-sized Mjolnir". A Mjolnir *inside* or centred on a disc would therefore look like Doom's clasp. https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/doctor-doom-cloak-fasteners-in-avengers-doomsday-explored (opened)
- Thor is Stormbreaker, so no Thor clash.
- Sam's mark is a disc with level wings. The hammer reads on a diagonal, so the outlines differ.

##### Recommendation

**Primary: (c) the round shield with Mjolnir crossed behind it.**
- Silhouette: the shield disc, slightly smaller than full frame, with Mjolnir behind it on a 45° diagonal. The hammer head breaks well outside the rim at the upper right, and the strapped handle end protrudes at the lower left. The hammer must break the outline. Never place it inside the disc or centred on it (Doom's knot clasp).
- Cut-outs: (1) a five-pointed star at the shield's centre, kept small (DESIGN note on the Captain Marvel star read); (2) one concentric ring slot. Optionally, (3) a thin slot separating the hammer head from the rim so the two objects read as two.
- Why: it is the only option that is both Steve's highest-recognition image (Endgame) and his Doomsday image (trailer, toy, promo). Against Sam it adds a diagonal weapon and no wings.

**Alternative: (a) the heater shield** (star cut-out plus 2-3 vertical stripe slots), as in the earlier pass. It is unique in outline and safe from every collision. But it recalls a stage prop that was dented and replaced, and casual viewers are unlikely to recognise it. Use it if the owner wants the shield family kept free of Mjolnir altogether.

**Reject (b)**, which cannot be told from Sam, and **(d)**, which is illegible at 16 px.

**Confidence: Medium-High** for (c) as the best-recognised option. The residual risk is the Doom knot-clasp similarity, which the off-axis, outline-breaking hammer is meant to defeat.

**Doomsday status.**
- Unconfirmed. Olsen has repeatedly said she is not in it.
- A reported *VisionQuest* post-credits scene (finale aired 10 November 2026) shows Doom saying "Scarlet Witch, awaken". It is sourced only to a fan account's clip. https://clutchpoints.com/entertainment/mcu-news-visionquest-post-credits-scene-return-avengers-doomsday (opened, 11 November 2026)
- Treat this as a strong rumour, not confirmation.

##### Separation analysis

| Neighbour | Its read | What Wanda must not share |
|---|---|---|
| Loki | A band with two tall, symmetrical horns rising up and out | Tall upward pair as the dominant feature |
| Sylvie | One horn plus a broken stub; asymmetric | Asymmetry |
| Namora | A fan of 5-7 straight upward spines | Many upward points |
| Magneto | A solid dome, a crest dipping to the brow, cheek guards past the jaw, a face opening | A solid dome; a frame reaching the jaw |

The one feature none of them has is **downward points**: the brow drop and the two cheek tips, framing an open face. Loki, Sylvie and Namora point only up. Magneto has a downward crest, but on a solid dome. Wanda's crown is an open, thin frame: Park's "negative space".

##### Recommendation

**Primary: the face-framing crown, front view.**
- Silhouette: a symmetric open frame, wider than tall.
  - Two *short* upswept points at the top corners, deliberately shorter than the frame is wide, so they never read as horns.
  - The centre of the top edge dips into a V that continues down as a pointed drop onto the brow.
  - The side arms curve down and end in points at cheekbone height (the MoM length, not the jaw).
  - No dome: the top edge is open between the points.
- Cut-outs: (1) the large face opening, an inverted-heart shape bounded by the drop and the cheek arms. It is the main identity. (2) One small jewel at the drop: a teardrop, not a diamond, to stay clear of the seal's corner-on squares. (3) Optionally, one swirl slot in each side arm for Park's hex swirls (Level 2 only; too fine at 16 px).
- Rule for designers: three downward tips (drop and two cheeks) must outweigh two upward tips.

**Alternative: the comic full frame.** The frame runs to the jaw and encircles the face, as in the unused MoM variants and the classic headpiece. It is more comic-faithful, but it closes into a hood or face outline near Magneto's cheek guards and Doom's hood, so it is weaker on this roster.

**Confidence: Medium.** The MoM tiara's cheekbone length and two top points come from one trade article, plus Park's top, bottom and centre description. No source gives an exact count of internal points. Her Doomsday presence is unconfirmed.

##### Assessment of the shared base

**Quatrefoil, four round lobes.**
- *For:* it is a one-silhouette, unmistakably four-part shape. It recalls the four-module Fantasticar and the First Steps "accelerating curves", and it is new to the roster.
- *Risks:*
  - (1) **Swastika:** none, provided it keeps *mirror* symmetry (four identical, symmetric lobes). Swastika risk comes only from *chiral* four-fold shapes: hooked, bent or leaning arms that rotate one way. **Johnny's flames must therefore rise vertically. Never lean the four flame lobes in a pinwheel.** (Side note: Doctor Strange's recommended mark, "four swooping curved slots … in rotational symmetry", is exactly that chiral four-fold form and should be checked for the same risk.)
  - (2) **Square cross / Mjolnir-knot:** gaps between lobes that meet at the centre would draw a "+" or "×". Keep a solid hub, and make any seams stop short of the centre.
  - (3) **Seal:** never cut a square or diamond at the centre (the seal's nested squares), and never surround it with a ring.
  - (4) Minor: a four-leaf clover or Gothic tracery read. In faction context it reads as "four of one".

**Alternatives considered.**

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Disc split into quadrants | Draws a cross; also joins the crowded disc family. Reject |
| Four separate pods (2×2 dots) | Breaks "one silhouette"; reads as a dotted grid near the seal's dotted ring. Reject |
| Four-pointed star | Near Doom's rayed disc and the Captain Marvel star read. Reject |
| Rounded square | Near the seal's nested square. Reject |
| Quatrefoil on the diagonals (×-orientation) | Fine, but the gaps form a "+", which is closer to the cross |

Use the orthogonal orientation (lobes at north, east, south and west), which leaves notches on the diagonals, the classic Gothic form.

##### Recommendation

**Primary: one quatrefoil family, each member changing the whole outline in one way.**
- Shared base: a filled quatrefoil (four equal round lobes at north, east, south and west) with a solid hub.
- Shared cut-out: one small round eye at the hub. A single circle, no ring around it. Each member adds 1-2 cut-outs of their own.
- **Reed:** the quatrefoil stretched sideways to about 2.5:1. The east and west lobes become long rounded arms and the north and south lobes stay small. Cut-out: the hub eye, drawn out into a horizontal slot.
- **Sue:** the left half solid. The right two lobes dissolve into 2-3 vertical slats thinning to nothing (invisibility), or are drawn as a thin outline (force field). Prefer slats: an outline-only lobe risks a ring read. Cut-outs: the hub eye plus the slat gaps.
- **Johnny:** each lobe's top drawn up into a flame tongue. **All four flames rise vertically**, with the north lobe tallest. No rotational lean. Cut-outs: the hub eye plus one teardrop gap.
- **Ben:** each lobe faceted into a straight-edged polygon, so the quatrefoil becomes a cluster of four rocks. Cut-outs: the hub eye plus 1-2 short crack lines that stop short of the hub, so no cross forms.

**Alternative: the four-module Fantasticar seen from above.** Four rounded, teardrop pods tucked tail-in around a central hub, with mirror symmetry and pods pointing outward on the orthogonals. It is the same safety logic as the quatrefoil with a direct comics source. It is less familiar to film-only viewers, and the First Steps car does not split on screen.

**Confidence: Medium.** No source gives the film a non-numeral FF symbol, so the base is our invention and its "FF" read depends on faction grouping. The per-member variations are well grounded (powers and First Steps' own per-member logos). The swastika and cross risks are fully controllable with the rules above.
